Privacy Policy

Tower Masters: Eternal Defence

Effective date: 6 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Tower Masters: Eternal Defence ("the Game").

Tower Masters: Eternal Defence is an Android tower defence game. The Game does not require you to create a separate Tower Masters account. Some optional features use Google services, including Google Play Games, Google Play Billing and Google AdMob.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The Game stores certain gameplay information locally on your device so that your progress can be remembered between sessions.

This may include:

  • Campaign progress
  • Completed maps and difficulty progress
  • Player level and experience
  • Gems and campaign lives
  • Tower unlocks
  • Crests and cosmetic unlocks
  • Achievements
  • Endless Mode progress and best wave
  • Game settings such as music, sound effects and haptics
  • Tutorial and onboarding progress

This local information can normally be removed by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game from your device.

2. Google Play Games

Tower Masters: Eternal Defence supports Google Play Games services. If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, Google may process information associated with your Google Play Games profile.

This may include:

  • Your Google Play Games player name and player identifier
  • Leaderboard scores
  • Achievements
  • Player level information submitted to leaderboards
  • Saved game information and game progress

Google Play Games may also allow game progress to be restored or synchronised between supported devices.

Google handles this information according to Google's own privacy policies and account controls.

3. Advertising

Tower Masters: Eternal Defence uses Google AdMob to provide advertising.

The Game may contain:

  • Interstitial advertisements shown at appropriate points in gameplay
  • Optional rewarded advertisements that may provide an additional campaign life

Google Mobile Ads may automatically process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • Device and advertising identifiers
  • Advertising interactions
  • App interactions such as taps and ad views
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention

The exact advertising experience may depend on your region, privacy choices, device settings and applicable law.

4. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, the Game may use Google's consent tools, including the Google User Messaging Platform, to request or manage advertising consent and privacy choices.

Users may also have additional advertising and privacy controls available through their Google account and Android device settings.

5. Ad-Free Purchase

Tower Masters: Eternal Defence may offer an optional one-time purchase that removes automatic interstitial advertisements.

Optional rewarded advertisements may remain available to players who choose to watch them in exchange for an in-game reward.

6. In-App Purchases

Tower Masters: Eternal Defence may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play.

Payments are processed by Google Play. The developer of Tower Masters: Eternal Defence does not directly receive or store your full payment-card details.

Google may process purchase information, account information and transaction details according to Google's own policies.

The Game may receive confirmation that a purchase has been successfully completed so that the appropriate in-game content or entitlement can be provided.

7. Information We Do Not Directly Collect

Tower Masters: Eternal Defence does not operate its own external user-account database and does not directly request information such as:

  • Your home address
  • Your telephone number
  • Your payment-card details
  • Your contacts
  • Your photographs or videos
  • Your microphone recordings
  • Your precise GPS location

8. Third-Party Services

Tower Masters: Eternal Defence may use the following Google services:

  • Google Play Games Services
  • Google Play Games Saved Games
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads
  • Google User Messaging Platform

These services may process information independently under Google's privacy terms.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

9. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with Google where necessary to provide features such as advertising, purchases, leaderboards, saved games, achievements, fraud prevention and other Google Play services.

10. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the Game. Information transmitted to Google services is handled using the security measures provided by those services.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game progress remains on your device until it is removed, the Game's data is cleared, or the Game is uninstalled.

Information saved through Google Play Games or other Google services may be retained according to Google's policies and your Google account settings.

You can manage or delete information associated with your Google account using Google's account and Play Games privacy controls.

Because Tower Masters: Eternal Defence does not create a separate developer- operated user account, there is no separate Tower Masters account that needs to be deleted.

12. Children's Privacy

Tower Masters: Eternal Defence is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

The developer does not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly to the developer.

Google services used by the Game may apply additional age, consent and privacy protections where required by applicable law and Google policies.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the Game, third-party services, legal requirements or privacy practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page and the effective date will be updated where appropriate.
